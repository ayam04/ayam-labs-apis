01 - WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

Detection, then a policy you choose

mask

Keeps the last four characters. What a support agent needs to read a card back.

label

Stable tokens like [EMAIL_1]. Two occurrences share a token, so a model can follow the thread.

hash

SHA-256 prefix plus a count map. Records stay joinable, the value never comes back.

validators

Luhn for cards, mod-97 for IBANs. A number that fails the check is not reported as a card.
