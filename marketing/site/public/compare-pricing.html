The one that matters: bulk verification

A single verification at $0.05 is ten times what a credit-pack vendor charges. The bulk endpoint changes that completely: it takes up to 100 addresses per call, so the same $0.05 buys 100 checks, which is $0.0005 per address. That is roughly ten times cheaper than the cheapest credit pack we found (EmailListVerify, €5 per 1,000 = €0.005 each).

If you are verifying one address at a time, we are the wrong tool and we would rather say so. If you are cleaning a list, the bulk path is the cheapest per-address price in this comparison.

Where we are expensive on purpose

DocParse bills per call, not per page. A 100-page PDF is $0.0005 per page, at parity with the cheapest OCR service. A one-page file is $0.05 per page, which is 66 times the market. If your documents are always short, per-page pricing elsewhere will beat us.

TextMind runs deterministic methods (sentiment, keywords, entities, language) and neural ones (summaries, rewrites, labels, translation) behind the same call. At $0.05 the neural endpoints are fine; the deterministic ones are cheap to compute and priced high, which is why there is no free tier and no reason to run them on every row.
