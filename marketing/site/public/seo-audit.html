01 - ENDPOINTS

8 endpoints, one key

Every route below is described in the OpenAPI document. Import it into Postman or generate a client.

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/v1/auditFull on-page audit plus a weighted score breakdown.
GET/v1/metaTitle, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph, Twitter, JSON-LD.
GET/v1/linksInternal vs external links, anchor texts, rel attributes.
GET/v1/keywordsTerm frequency, TF-IDF ranking and density for a page or raw text.
GET/v1/readabilityFlesch, Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning fog, sentence stats.
GET/v1/robotsParsed robots.txt groups, rules, crawl-delay, sitemaps.
GET/v1/sitemapSitemap index or urlset, parsed and counted.
POST/v1/textReadability plus keyword analysis on raw text.

03 - WHY

Built for the boring part

No headless browser bill

HTMLRewriter parses the document as a stream. You get structured findings in one pass instead of paying for a Chrome instance per page.

Checks you can act on

Every check returns pass, warn or fail with its weight and a message. Change one field, watch the score move by a known amount.

SSRF safe by default

Every fetch is resolved first and rejected if it points at a private address, on every redirect hop. Safe to expose behind a public form.

06 - FAQ

Questions we get asked

Will it work on JavaScript-heavy sites?

You get the server-rendered HTML. Single-page apps that render entirely in the browser will look thin. That is a real signal too, but treat the numbers accordingly.

Can I audit a local or intranet URL?

No. Private ranges, localhost and metadata endpoints are blocked twice: on hostname pattern and on DNS resolution of every redirect hop. That is deliberate.

How is the score calculated?

Each check carries a fixed weight and returns pass, warn or fail. The score is the weighted sum normalised to 0-100, and the full breakdown is in the response so you can explain it to a client.

Do you cache pages?

No page bodies are stored. DNS answers are cached for 10 minutes at the edge.
