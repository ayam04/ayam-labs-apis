without the fog.

Short version: the marketplace handles billing, you keep ownership of what you send, we do not store your payloads, and you agree not to use these APIs to break the law or someone else's terms.

01 - SERVICE

What you are buying

A subscription to one or more HTTP APIs operated by Ayam Labs. Access is granted through the RapidAPI marketplace, which issues keys, enforces quotas and processes payments. Availability targets are best effort; there is no uptime SLA on the self-serve plans. Custom SLAs are available for commits above the Mega tier.

Responses are provided as-is. Verification, scoring, sentiment and detection results are heuristics. Do not use them as the sole basis for a decision that materially affects a person.

02 - ACCEPTABLE USE

What you cannot do

You agree not to:

  • resell raw API output as a competing API or dataset;
  • circumvent quotas, share keys publicly, or automate signup to farm free tiers;
  • use the fetch-based endpoints to attack, scan or overload third party servers;
  • submit content you have no right to process, or use the APIs to build a system that harasses people;
  • use the PII detectors as a substitute for a regulated security control.

Automated abuse, credential stuffing via the verification endpoints, or attempts to reach private networks through the fetching endpoints result in immediate suspension.

03 - PRIVACY

What we store

Request payloads are not stored. Email addresses, documents, page bodies and text are held in memory for the duration of the request and discarded. There is no queue, no bucket and no database of customer content.

What is logged, for up to 30 days: the route, HTTP status, duration, a random request id, the API key id and coarse request metadata. This is used for debugging and quota accounting.

Third party requests: EmailCheck queries public DNS resolvers. SEO Audit and DocParse fetch the URL you ask them to fetch, from Cloudflare's network, with your request as the cause. TextMind sends text to Cloudflare Workers AI only for the neural endpoints you explicitly call; the deterministic endpoints never leave the edge.

Personal data: if you send personal data to a detector, the response contains it back to you (that is the point). We do not build profiles, and we do not sell data.

04 - LIABILITY AND CHANGES

The boring part

Liability is limited to the amount you paid in the previous three months. These terms may change; material changes are announced on this page at least 14 days before taking effect. Continued use after the effective date is acceptance.

Governed by the laws applicable at the operator's place of business. Disputes go to the marketplace's process first.
