02 - COSTS

What actually costs us money

DNS and HTTP lookups

EmailCheck and SEO Audit make live network calls per unique domain or URL. Those are cached at the edge for 10 minutes, which is why the free tier can be generous.

Neural calls

TextMind's AI endpoints bill per token to Cloudflare Workers AI. They are the only endpoints where cost scales with your input length, and they are capped per request because of it.

Everything else

PDF parsing, PII detection, keyword extraction, redaction and scoring all run on the CPU of a single Worker. Marginal cost is effectively zero, which is why those plans can carry large quotas.

03 - FAQ

Billing questions

Who charges my card?

The RapidAPI marketplace. Your subscription, invoice, overage and refund all live there. We never see your card details.

What happens when I hit my quota?

The marketplace starts counting overages at the published rate, and warns you at 85% and 100%. If you would rather hard stop than pay overage, set a hard limit on the plan.

Can I move between plans mid-month?

Yes, from the listing page. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades on the next cycle.

Do you offer annual or volume pricing?

For commits above the Mega tier, email us and we will publish a custom plan. For everything else the published ladder is the whole price list.

What if an endpoint returns a 5xx?

Failed requests are not counted as usage. If a bad deploy breaks your integration, tell us and we will credit the affected period.
